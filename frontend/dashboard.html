HPSee workflows

Work sent to the BioSolveIT HPSee server, as HPSee reports it. Each workflow is made of tasks (for example Docking, then Annotation). HPSee gives each task's progress when it finishes, so a bar may stay at 0% and then jump to 100%. That is HPSee's own figure, not an estimate.

Batches

Work is split into batches of molecules. Each batch is saved when it finishes, so an interruption never repeats finished batches. A failed batch is retried automatically a few times; after that it stays here for you to inspect and retry.
